Cherry cola hair color is a deep brown with a red-cherry glow that shows up in the light. Pinterest's first Beauty Trend Report lists cherry cola brunette searches up 788% year on year, so it is one of the biggest fall hair colors right now. Here is what it is, who it suits and how to keep it from fading.

What is cherry cola hair color?

Think of a dark chocolate brown with a cherry-red tint running through it. In dim light it looks like rich brunette. In sunlight or under a lamp, the red shows. It is softer than a bold red and easier to grow out, which is why many people pick it for fall and winter.

Cherry cola shades to ask for

  • Classic cherry cola: dark brown with an even red-violet tint.
  • Soft cherry brown: a lighter brown with a subtle red glow, good if you want to start small.
  • Cherry cola balayage: a brown base with red hand-painted around the face and ends.
  • Black cherry: almost black with a deeper wine-red flash.

Who does it suit?

Cherry cola works on most dark and medium brown hair, because it builds on the color you already have. Warm skin tones often look best with a red-copper lean. Cool and neutral skin tones tend to suit the red-violet side. If you are not sure, take two photos to your stylist and ask which direction fits your skin tone.

What to ask your stylist

  1. Bring two or three photos of the shade you like, in daylight.
  2. Say "cherry cola brunette" and name your starting color and any past dye.
  3. Ask whether you need lightening first. On dark hair, a gloss or semi-permanent color can often add the red without it.
  4. Ask how often the color needs a refresh, and what home products they trust.

How to keep cherry cola hair from fading

Red tones fade faster than brown. A few habits slow it down:

  • Wash with cool water and a sulfate-free, color-safe shampoo.
  • Use a color-depositing conditioner in a red or copper tint once a week.
  • Wash less often and use a heat protectant when you style.
  • Book a gloss between color appointments if the red looks dull.

Cherry cola hair color FAQ

Is cherry cola hair color high maintenance? It is medium maintenance. The brown base grows out gently, but the red fades and needs refreshing.

Does it work on dark hair without bleach? Often yes, with a gloss or semi-permanent color. Your stylist can tell you after looking at your hair.

When is the best time to get it? Fall and winter, when deep, warm tones feel right and the trend is at its peak.
